Resume Projects

I'm a software and gameplay developer with a background in both game development and enterprise software. I specialize in the systems that make interactive experiences feel right — player interactions, event sequencing, tools, and the feedback loops that turn good games into memorable ones.

From 2020 to 2023, I built AR and VR experiences at the UC Center for Simulation and Virtual Environments, working across the full development lifecycle on projects ranging from medical training simulations to Hope After Hate, an interactive experience about the life of a Holocaust survivor. Since graduating from the University of Cincinnati in 2025, I've been working as a software developer at London Computer Systems, building and maintaining backend systems for property management software used by thousands of companies.

When I'm not working, I'm probably adding features to BookHarbour, painting, tending to my garden, or gaming with friends.
